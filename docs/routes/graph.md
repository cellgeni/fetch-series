# The route graph

Every link between records, files and documents the archives offer, and what
is known about each. Columns are archives — GEO, NCBI, EBI, DDBJ, ArrayExpress —
and rows are levels, from datasets down to read files, supplementary files and
metadata documents. A **measured** route is a solid line: its ink is the share
of its source's queried accessions its survey resolved, its width how many of
them reached this target. A **surveyed** link is a hollow slate line of one
width: a link survey's seeded draw found it usable, and it carries counts and
never a rank. An **untested** link is a thin dotted line with a hollow
arrowhead: no survey has measured it, and one live request found it answering —
and, into a file or document, found something usable.

## How to read it

**Measured routes / Measured + surveyed / All known links** switches between
the declared routes alone, the routes and every link a link survey measured,
and every link [the catalogue](../graph/catalogue.md) has confirmed. All is the
default, because the gaps between the routes are the point of drawing
it. Submissions, analyses and GEO platforms are hidden until asked for: they
are records *about* the data, not levels of it, and they cross every row.

### Columns, colours and shapes

Each archive's records sit in its own column, and **colour repeats the
column**: GEO orange, NCBI blue, EBI green, DDBJ violet, ArrayExpress amber.
Neighbouring columns are told apart by every reader, colour-blind ones
included; columns two apart are not always (NCBI blue and DDBJ violet), which
is why every label also names its archive — *SRA run*, *ENA run*, *DRA run* —
and carries the accession prefix under it. A BioProject or BioSample is one
record held by all three INSDC archives, and sits on a row of its own above
its level, within their columns. Read files belong to no archive: they are grouped by format, and grey.

Shape says what a node is: a stack of sheets for a dataset, a capsule for a
sample, a hexagon for a library, a slanted bar for a run, a plain card for a
record about the data, a folded page for a file, and a card with a ruled header
for a metadata document.

### A measured route

A declared route is drawn **once per node pair it covers**: BioProject → SRR,
→ ERR and → DRR are three lines of `bioproject->run:ena_filereport`. Each is
cut from the route's own recorded verdicts, by the archive of each accession
and the format of each file — filtered measurement, never new evidence
([a route's split is not new evidence](../decisions/what-counts-as-evidence.md#a-routes-split-is-not-new-evidence)).
Where several routes answer the same direction they run side by side, in the
order the resolver tries them: `RouteRegistry.ranked()` over the evidence, not a
hand-written preference ([how routes are ranked](../decisions/route-ranking.md)).

- **Ink** is the share of the source's queried accessions that resolved — SRRs
  alone, for a line from SRA runs — in five steps from pale (under 25%) to full
  ink (95% or more).
- **Width** is how many of those accessions had a result on this target, on a
  log scale.
- **Hatched** marks the six routes measured by the 2026-05 scripts, which kept a
  single boolean per BioProject that lumped empty in with failed. Their share
  cannot be computed, and is drawn as unknown rather than guessed.
- A line into a read format says **who made the files** in brackets: submitted,
  generated by the archive, an SRA object, or mixed.

A pair the survey never queried is not drawn as measured: it is probed like any
other link. A pair the survey queried and never reached is a *measured
absence*: listed in the panels and the matrix, with no line. Hover or click a
route for its split and the whole route's evidence.

### A surveyed link

One hollow line per node pair where a link survey found something usable: the
probe's request made over a seeded draw of 300 of the source node's accessions,
or all of them where there are fewer
([link surveys](../graph/catalogue.md#link-surveys)). Clicking it lists each
mechanism with, per corpus, how many accessions it found something **usable**
for out of how many were queried, the resolved, empty and failed counts, the
distinct values, each check's tally, and the date.

It has one width whatever it found, and no rank, on purpose. A draw of 300 puts
up to ±5.7 points on a yield, which says whether a link answers always,
sometimes or never and cannot say which of two answers better
([a link survey is not a ranking](../decisions/what-counts-as-evidence.md#a-link-survey-is-measurement-not-a-ranking)).
A link whose survey found nothing usable is not drawn; it is listed in the
panels as *surveyed, never usable*.

### An untested link

One dotted line per node pair, however many mechanisms answer it. Their names
appear along the line once you zoom in, and in full on hover. Clicking it lists
each mechanism's request, the field the link is read from, whether the archive
documents that field, and the probe that confirmed it: which accession, on which
day, and what came back.

A line into a read format is drawn only where the file found is **valid**: it
downloads at the size the archive published, the archive publishes its md5 and
size, and it is complete for 10x — the barcode read and the cDNA read both
there. A supplementary file must download and be size-checkable, a metadata
document download and parse. The panel lists each check and what it said.

A probe proves a link exists and **measures nothing**, so an untested line
carries no counts, no rank and no ink
([a probe is not evidence](../decisions/what-counts-as-evidence.md#a-probe-is-not-evidence)).
A documented link whose probe found nothing, or found something that failed a
check, is not drawn; it is listed in the panels and on
[the catalogue page](../graph/catalogue.md#gaps).

### Panels, matrix and paths

Clicking a node lists everything it **reaches in one hop**, with what is known
about each, and every node it has **no one-hop link to**. The matrix under the
graph is the same information for every pair at once: filled where a route
measures the pair, ringed where a link survey does and no route, outlined where
only untested links do, struck through where a route measured an absence or a
survey found nothing usable, dashed where a link is documented and not
confirmed, blank where nothing links them. Clicking a cell selects that pair.

Paths are node paths of up to four hops. Over **measured routes** the path
finder takes, for each hop, the best-ranked route the filters leave visible,
which is what the resolver would try first. In **measured + surveyed** it also
uses surveyed links, where no route answers the hop. With **include untested**
it also offers paths through untested links: each pair's shortest paths and
those one hop longer.

The data behind this page is printed by `uv run fetch routes graph`, which
produces it the same way the docs build does. Node positions were searched, not
placed by hand, by `scripts/route_graph_layout.py`, within each node's row and
archive column. The same search then bends a bundle that would pass under a
node it does not belong to, but only where a bend clears every such node:
in the default view that leaves 23 route crossings of that kind and 28 untested
ones, against 32 and 94 drawn with one gentle curve for all. What is left is
mostly BioProject's and BioSample's lines, which reach almost every node below
them. Letting those two sit outside the INSDC columns was tried, and the
search put both back where they were. Colours were chosen by running the categorical palette through
a validator in both themes: every pair of neighbouring columns separates for
colour-blind readers, and the pairs that do not are never next to each other.
